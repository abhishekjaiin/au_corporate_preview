# Off-page plan (from the audit's "Low SERP visibility" finding)

The audit found that neither theaucorp.com nor krprassociates.com ranks in the top 9 for any of the
15 head terms tested, and that the ranking sites (vjmglobal, kanakkupillai, setindiabiz,
india-briefing, treelife, ebizfiling, registerkaro, ascgroup) win on authority more than on content.
The on-site work is now done; this plan covers the outreach that only the AU team can do.

## 1. Targets

Membership, listing and sponsorship decisions are AU's own; confirm each before acting. Do not claim
any membership or partnership on the site until it exists.

| Type | Targets | What to offer | Page to link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bilateral chambers and business councils | US-India Business Council (USIBC), UK India Business Council (UKIBC), JETRO India, Indo-German Chamber of Commerce (IGCC), Indo-Australian and Singapore business chambers, Dutch and Nordic chambers in India, India-UAE business councils | Member directory listing, a guest article for their newsletter, a webinar on setting up in India | The matching country hub, e.g. /usa-to-india, /uk-to-india, /japan-to-india, /germany-to-india, /uae-to-india, /europe-to-india |
| Professional directories | Clutch, GoodFirms, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page, relevant ICAI/ICSI firm listings where available | Complete profile, service categories, client reviews | /, /india-subsidiary-registration |
| Cross-border publications and blogs | Expansion, payroll, EOR and international tax blogs; trade association newsletters; university entrepreneurship centres | Guest articles built on the site's guides (one topic per article) | The guide the article is based on |
| Data-led PR | Business press in the parent countries | Short data notes, e.g. setup timelines by country, the 50% wage rule impact, Press Note 3 easing | /how-long-to-set-up-a-subsidiary-in-india, /new-labour-codes-india-50-percent-wage-rule-gratuity, /press-note-3-approval-india |
| Partners | Law firms, banks, relocation and EOR firms abroad that refer India work | Reciprocal resource pages, co-authored guides | /partners pages, country hubs |

## 2. Pitch templates

**Chamber newsletter / guest article**

> Subject: Article offer: setting up an Indian subsidiary from [country] in 2026
>
> Dear [name], AU Corporate is a firm of Chartered Accountants and Company Secretaries that sets up
> and runs Indian subsidiaries for foreign companies. We have published a detailed guide for
> [country] companies ([link]) and would be glad to write an original 800-1,000 word article for
> your members on [topic: e.g. the India-[country] tax treaty, posting staff to India, FEMA filings].
> No promotional content beyond a short author bio. Would this be useful for an upcoming issue?

**Directory review request (existing clients)**

> Thank you for working with us on your Indian company. If you have two minutes, a short review on
> [Clutch / Google] would help other foreign companies find us: [link]. Thank you.

**Data note for press**

> [One-paragraph finding, e.g. "Most foreign subsidiaries in India take 4-6 weeks to incorporate,
> but the bank account is now the slowest step"], two supporting points, a quote from a partner, and
> a link to the full guide.

## 3. 90-day schedule

| Weeks | Actions |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Complete Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Clutch and GoodFirms profiles; ask 5-10 clients for reviews |
| 3-6 | Contact 2 chambers per week, starting with the countries with the most traffic potential (USA, Japan, Germany, UK); offer one article each |
| 5-8 | Publish 2-4 guest articles; each links to one country hub or guide |
| 7-10 | Send 2 data notes to business press in parent countries |
| 9-12 | Review results in Search Console and Ahrefs; repeat for the next countries (Singapore, Australia, UAE, Europe) |

## 4. Tracking

Keep a sheet with: target, contact, date contacted, offer, status, live URL, linked page, and
anchor text. Review rankings for the audit's 15 head terms monthly.

## 5. Reviews and schema

The audit recommends Review / AggregateRating schema only once real reviews exist. When genuine
reviews are collected on a platform, tell the developer which reviews may be shown; schema must match
reviews visible on the page.
